# Code of conduct

Orchestraty is built in the open. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is welcome, as long as they help keep this a place where people want to work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to coding agents.
- Disagree about the work, not the person: say what you think is wrong with a change, and why.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Accept that the maintainers decide what is merged, by [the rules](docs/rules.md).
- Credit other people's work, and keep private what was shared in private.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, an email or anything from a private report, without their permission.
- Derailing a thread on purpose, or encouraging any of the above.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and releases, and wherever someone speaks for the project in public.

## Reporting a problem

Report it privately on this repository's **Security** tab: choose **Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainers see it. Say what happened, where (links help) and when. The maintainers read every report, answer in it, and keep who reported it private.

## What the maintainers may do

Depending on what happened: a private word, a public note on the thread, editing or removing comments, or a ban from the project, for a while or for good. They say why.

This code takes its ideas from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
